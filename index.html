Mechanical Engineering at RGIPT Jais

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology, Jais, focuses on developing strong theoretical, practical and research-oriented knowledge in mechanical engineering.

The department provides students with knowledge and practical exposure in areas including Thermal and Pipeline Engineering, Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Industrial Engineering.

The department aims to prepare students with the technical skills required for modern industries, energy applications and advanced engineering research.
