NEXT: GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE
1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Add a URL-prefix property for:
   https://rohit14rgipt.github.io/rgipt-mechanical-jais/
3. Choose HTML tag verification.
4. Google will give you a meta tag containing a unique verification value.
5. Add that exact meta tag inside index.html <head>.
6. Deploy the updated site to GitHub Pages.
7. Return to Search Console and click Verify.
8. Open Sitemaps and submit:
   https://rohit14rgipt.github.io/rgipt-mechanical-jais/sitemap.xml
9. Use URL Inspection for the homepage and request indexing.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not put the Google verification value in chat or share it publicly.
- Search indexing is controlled by Google; submitting the sitemap does not guarantee a particular ranking or immediate indexing.
